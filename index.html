FAQ

My purchase didn't arrive.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID, then reopen the app. For "Remove Ads", open the Store and tap "Restore Purchases".

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress is saved only on your device; there is no cloud save yet.

How do I turn off sounds, music or vibration?

Tap the gear on the main menu, or use the toggles in the pause menu.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Main menu → Settings → Privacy options (shown where consent is required). Tracking can also be turned off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
